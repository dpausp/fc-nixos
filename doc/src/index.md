## Platform Editions & Releases

Our platform documentation is versioned to match your active environment. We actively maintain **three releases** at any time:
* **Stable:** The current production-ready platform release.
* **Sunsetting:** The two previous releases still under maintenance support.

> **Tip:** Use the **version selector in the top-right navigation** to switch to the documentation matching your deployed release.

For detailed migration paths and release history, see the **[Platform What's new and Upgardes](./infrastructure/upgrades-whats-new.md)**.
