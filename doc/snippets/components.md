Components (roles) are preconfigured, automated service modules managed by Flying Circus on our NixOS-based cloud platform. They serve as building blocks for assembling complex, scalable, and production-ready application environments. Rather than manually installing, configuring, and maintaining software components, developers and administrators can leverage these roles to declaratively provision the desired infrastructure. In projects with a Managed or Guided service level, certain component roles directly impact billing and are therefore marked with a symbol (⚙︎) in the my.flyingcircus.io portal{:target="_blank"}.
